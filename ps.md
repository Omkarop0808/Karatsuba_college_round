.Verity: Prove the Computation, Not Just the Result Backstory: Organizations increasingly rely on external services to perform computations and process sensitive data. However, verifying whether a service actually performed the agreed computation correctly can be difficult without independently repeating the entire process. Recording results alone does not guarantee their correctness, creating a need for verifiable and tamper-evident computation. Problem Statement: Develop Verity, a blockchain-based verifiable computation platform that enables one party to request a computation from another and independently verify that the returned result follows the agreed rules. The system should establish trust between parties without requiring them to blindly rely on the computation provider or repeat expensive computations themselves. Key Features: Verifiable Computation: Establish mechanisms to prove that computational results follow the agreed execution rules. Independent Verification: Allow users to validate results without blindly trusting the provider. Tamper-Evident Records: Maintain verifiable records of computation requests, results, and verification outcomes. Input and Output Integrity: Ensure that the data and results associated with a computation can be independently authenticated. Dispute Resolution: Support challenges against suspicious results using verifiable evidence. Adversarial Testing: Demonstrate how the system detects manipulated outputs, invalid proofs, or inconsistencies between claimed and verified results. Execution Traceability: Provide a transparent history of computation requests, proof generation, and verification decisions
